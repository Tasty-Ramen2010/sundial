SUNDIAL — changes made on November 14, 2026

Regional fire-season explorer
Draw a rectangle on a country map or enter coordinates to view that area's
typical burning months. Country summaries show peak months, the selected
month's difference from its baseline, and the calibration basis. Shared links
preserve the country, month and selected box.

Local backend
Added a Python server with a regional-history endpoint and validated import
of harmonized monthly grid records. Start with: python3 server.py --port 8000.
The checked-in spatial data supports seasonal averages only. Regional historical
calendars and departures become available when real monthly records are loaded;
they are not estimated from country totals.

Faster access
Added a prominent calendar entry, searchable country suggestions, and a
one-click Angola example. Search opens the selected country's calendar directly.
Calendar shortcuts lead to the season summary, regional picker and methodology.

Sources and dates
Added NASA FIRMS links, coverage dates, calibration and validation descriptions,
baseline definitions, and uncertainty limitations. The page now distinguishes
historical observations (November 2000–December 2025) from the globe's 2012–2025
seasonal average and illustrative daily cycle. Selected-month source labels
identify MODIS or VIIRS. Exact product versions, upstream analysis revision and
interval construction remain explicitly unrecorded in this export.

Technical details and data-import instructions: README.md.
